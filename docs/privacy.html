Transparency & Non-Invasive Data Policy

Privacy Policy & Device Identifiers

Effective Date: October 2026 • Official Publisher: Zihan Fakir (xdm.zihan.xyz)

1. Core Principle: Zero Personal Data Collection

XDM Turbo respects your privacy. We believe a download manager should accelerate your downloads and get out of your way. We never collect, inspect, or log your download history, URLs, file contents, browser cookies, search history, or personal files. All download activity occurs strictly between your computer and the remote web server.

2. Why and How Device Identifiers Are Used

To provide single-seat and multi-seat commercial licenses without requiring invasive rootkits or dongles, XDM Turbo computes a stable, one-way pseudonym:

  • One-Way Pseudonymization: A cryptographic SHA-256 hash is computed using Windows machine attributes (MachineGuid and motherboard metadata). The raw hardware identifiers are discarded immediately and never transmitted.
  • Format: The transmitted device ID appears strictly as a 12-character format (e.g. XDM-DEV-A1B2-C3D4-E5F6).
  • Strict Purpose: This identifier is used exclusively to enforce the device seat limit (e.g. 1 device per purchased PRO license) and prevent duplicate fraudulent activations.

3. IP Address & Transient Security Logs

When activating a license key or performing an automated heartbeat validation, your IP address is received by our secure Vercel edge infrastructure:

  • IP addresses are utilized solely for rate-limiting (anti-brute-force defense) and blocking malicious botnets.
  • IP addresses are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • No passwords, private signing keys, or file paths are ever written to server audit logs.

4. Offline Grace Period & Heartbeats

XDM Turbo does not require a constant 24/7 internet connection to work. Once activated with a valid Ed25519 digital signature, the software maintains a generous 7-day offline grace period. If you travel or lose connectivity, PRO features remain fully unlocked for up to 7 consecutive days without contacting the server.

5. Device Deactivation & License Portability

If you purchase a new PC or reinstall Windows, you can transfer your license at any time. Simply deactivate your license within the desktop app or request an administrative slot release by contacting support on WhatsApp or Telegram (@zihanfakir).
